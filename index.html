What is Twinku?

Twinku is an asset database and inheritance planning app that offers financial intelligence & ensures your financial assets never get into the wrong hands.

Who can register on Twinku?

Can I operate the accounts I connect on Twinku?

No, you cannot operate your connected accounts on Twinku. At such you cannot transfer, receive, make payment from & to your connected accounts from Twinku.
